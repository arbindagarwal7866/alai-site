Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026 · Contact alai.app.team@gmail.com

Alai is a student-made app for VIT students (Vellore and Bhopal). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or run by VIT.

The short version. Alai reads your VTOP data only while you sync, and keeps everything it learns on your phone. It has no server of its own, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What Alai reads, and when

Only when you tap Sync, and only after you have logged in to VTOP yourself, Alai opens the same "view" pages you can open in VTOP and reads:

  • attendance (including each class marked present or absent), timetable and academic calendar
  • digital assignments and their due dates
  • marks posted so far, and your grade history (past grades and CGPA)
  • each course's syllabus and books (once per semester)
  • club and chapter events from the VTOP home page
  • your faculty's cabin and email from VTOP's Faculty Information (once per semester)

You log in on VTOP's own page; Alai never reads your captcha and never sends your password anywhere except VTOP's own login page. With "Remember my login" on (the default; Settings → Login & lock), Alai keeps your register number and password on your phone, encrypted with Android's keystore, and fills them into VTOP's login page next time, so you only type the captcha. Turn it off or forget them any time. The VTOP page runs in a private window, so your VTOP session is gone from Alai when the sync ends. Alai never changes anything on VTOP.

What is kept, and where

  • Everything Alai reads, in a database inside Alai on your phone
  • What you add yourself: check-ins, your own deadlines, lab grade targets, settings
  • Your notes, board photos and PDFs, in Alai's own private folder
  • Phone numbers you add for your faculty

Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Alai, so this data is not copied to Google Drive either.

What leaves your phone

Where it goesWhat is sentWhy
Your campus's VTOP (vtop.vit.ac.in or vtop.vitbhopal.ac.in)Ordinary page requests from your own logged-in sessionTo read your data when you sync
Expo's update service (expo.dev)The app's version, update channel and a random install number. Nothing about you or your VTOP dataTo check for and download new versions of the app when it opens

Nothing else leaves your phone on its own. Image to PDF runs on the phone; your pictures are never uploaded. If a sync breaks, "Send a report" describes the VTOP page with every name, number and mark masked, and goes only where you choose to share it.

Alai never

  • sends your VTOP password anywhere except VTOP's own login page, or reads your captcha
  • changes anything on VTOP
  • uploads your data anywhere
  • shows ads, or uses analytics or trackers
  • reads your messages, mail, contacts or other apps

Permissions

  • Notifications: your morning brief, check-in, deadline and re-sync reminders. They are made on the phone, not sent from a server.
  • Camera: only when you tap the camera in Notes, to photograph the board.
  • Photos and files: only the photo or PDF you pick yourself.
  • Alarms: only when you tap "Set alarm", to add one alarm to your Clock app.
  • Fingerprint: only to unlock Alai. Your phone checks it and tells Alai just yes or no; Alai never sees your fingerprint. Your Alai PIN, if you set one, is kept only on your phone, encrypted by Android's keystore.
  • Mail and phone apps: only when you tap a faculty member's email or number.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete all my data erases everything, notes and files included. Uninstalling Alai does the same. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

Alai is meant for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and shown in the app before it applies. If a future feature ever needs to share something (such as a club events list), it will only be information that is not about you, and the app will tell you first.
