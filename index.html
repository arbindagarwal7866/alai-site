Your semester, worked out for you.

Alai reads your VTOP after you log in yourself, and turns it into plain answers: can you skip, what's due, what you can't miss. Everything stays on your phone.

In testing with VIT Vellore students · Android

Can I skip?

Every course shows how many classes you can still miss, until the end of the semester.

Don't-skip alarms

When one more miss could cost you before an exam, Alai tells you and sets an alarm in your Clock app.

Today

Your classes, what's due in the next 3 days and club events. From 9 pm, tomorrow.

Deadlines

VTOP digital assignments plus your own, with reminders 2 days before.

Marks

How many marks you can still lose for your target grade in each lab.

Notes

One space per subject for notes, board photos and PDFs, with search and the syllabus.

Private by design

You log in on VTOP's own page; Alai never sees or stores your password or captcha. Everything it reads stays on your phone: no account, no server, no ads, no tracking. Read exactly what Alai does.
